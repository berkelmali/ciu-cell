Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

At CIU-Cell, we respect your privacy. We only collect basic information necessary to provide and simulate our services, such as names, surnames, and comments submitted through our contact form. Payment information processed in our shopping portal is entirely simulated for educational purposes and is never stored, logged, or processed by real financial entities.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your queries, improve our website functionality, and deliver a smooth interactive simulation of our telecommunication packages. We will never sell, lease, or distribute your private information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Our website utilizes local storage (`localStorage`) to remember your theme preferences (Dark Mode or Light Mode) across page transitions. We do not use tracking pixels, cross-site advertisements, or intrusive third-party analytical cookies.

4. Security and Data Protection

We implement industry-standard front-end validation and clean script handling to ensure a secure local browsing experience. Since our website is fully client-side and simulated, we strongly advise against entering real credit card details or sensitive personal data.
